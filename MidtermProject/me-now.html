Biography

I am a junior at the University of Colorado Boulder studying Computer Science with a minor in Creative Technology and Design. My background growing up between the Philippines, Singapore, and the United States has shaped my appreciation for culture, community, and creativity. On campus, I currently serve as the Co-President of Asian Unity, where I help create spaces that bring students together and celebrate diverse identities. I am passionate about design and technology, and I enjoy building digital experiences that are both thoughtful and engaging. In the future, I hope to work in UI/UX design and help small businesses bring their ideas to life by designing and launching meaningful websites.
